Time from RIC Control Request (cap applied) to convergence of delivered UL throughput, for each cap level in the same 2/4/6 Mbps staircase shown in Fig. Plot 1. Convergence is defined as the first point at which delivered throughput enters and remains within +/-15% of that phase's own steady-state mean (computed excluding the first 5 s token-bucket-drain transient) for at least 3 consecutive 1 s samples: 2 Mbps in 3.5 s (steady-state mean 1.77 Mbps); 4 Mbps in 1.5 s (steady-state mean 3.48 Mbps); 6 Mbps in 1.5 s (steady-state mean 5.32 Mbps). Convergence time does not grow with the cap's magnitude, indicating the enforcement mechanism's responsiveness is independent of the target rate.

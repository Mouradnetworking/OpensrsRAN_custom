Uplink throughput for a single UE/DRB before, during, and after enforcement of a 4 Mbps per-DRB Maximum Flow Bit Rate (MFBR) cap, applied via O-RAN E2SM-RC Style 1 / Action 1 (DRB QoS Configuration RIC Control Request). The cap is applied at t = 45.0 s and removed at t = 90.0 s (45 s duration, shaded region). Offered load (green, dashed) is the UE-side iperf3 UDP send rate (1 s intervals), which is unaffected by the cap and averages 10.00 Mbps in the pre-cap window. Delivered throughput (blue, solid) is the core-side iperf3 receive rate (1 s intervals) and converges to a mean of 3.48 Mbps during the capped phase (excluding the first 5 s transient; 3.54 Mbps including it), against the 4 Mbps target. After cap removal, delivered throughput recovers to within 90% of the pre-cap offered rate within 3.5 s, reaching a mean of 9.14 Mbps over the following 10 s. No RRC Reconfiguration signaling, RLC/PDCP re-establishment, or connection failure occurred during the cap change.

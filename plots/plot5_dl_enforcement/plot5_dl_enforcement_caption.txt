Downlink throughput for a single UE/DRB before, during, and after enforcement of a 2 Mbps per-DRB Maximum Flow Bit Rate (MFBR) cap, applied via O-RAN E2SM-RC Style 1 / Action 1 (DRB QoS Configuration RIC Control Request), with iperf3 in reverse mode (offered load ~40.0 Mbps from the core-side server, ~20x oversubscribed against the cap). The cap is applied at t = 78.7 s and removed at t = 174.6 s (96 s duration, shaded region) - the second reconfiguration on the same DRB/UE session (apply, then remove) that previously crashed the connection reliably (see UL_MFBR_ENFORCEMENT_STATUS.md §2.3/§8) completes here with zero RLF and zero buffer-pool exhaustion, following two srsRAN_4G UE-side timer fixes (§8.1, §8.2). Delivered throughput converges to a mean of 2.19 Mbps during the capped phase (excluding the first 5 s transient; 2.17 Mbps including it), against the 2 Mbps target. After cap removal, delivered throughput recovers to within 90% of the pre-cap offered rate within 0.4 s, reaching a mean of 39.97 Mbps over the following 10 s.

Core-side UDP packet loss (%) for the same 2 Mbps cap window as Fig. Plot 1, from iperf3's receiver-computed `lost_percent` per 1 s interval (only the receiver can detect gaps in the UDP sequence numbers, so this is measured server-side, same source as the delivered-throughput line). Mean loss is 6.68% before the cap, rising to 81.08% while the 2 Mbps cap is active (offered load of ~10 Mbps exceeds the cap, so the excess is shed rather than buffered/delayed), and returns to 5.68% after the cap is removed. Mean jitter over the same window is 0.556 ms before the cap vs. 1.488 ms during enforcement.
